About the team

What is Pricing?

Pricing is the function that determines, governs, and executes how Goodyear brings its products to market at the right price, at the right time, for the right customer. The Pricing CR team sits within the global Pricing Administration organization and operates as a center of excellence spanning governance, operations, and commercial strategy.

From the moment a product price is decided at the factory, through master data updates, market-level adjustments, channel pricing, exception handling, and policy compliance — the Pricing team owns and executes every step of that journey.

How we operate

The Pricing Process — 6 Steps

Every price follows the same road, from the plant to the customer. Each step below maps to a numbered stop on the process flow.

1

Price Decided

Goodyear establishes a base price for each product, setting the foundation for all downstream pricing activities.

2

Master Data Updates

Pricing Specialists update and maintain pricing master data across systems to ensure accuracy and consistency.

3

Market & Country Pricing

Prices are adapted for local markets — including Minimum Advertised Pricing (MAP) and eCommerce channel requirements.

4

Channel Base Pricing

Base prices are set at the channel level, accounting for differences across wholesale, retail, fleet, and OE distribution.

5

Price Exceptions

Custom pricing is managed for Customer Programs, Deals (SCQs), Sales Programs, Rebates, and Competitive Pricing inputs.

6

Policy & Programs Exceptions

Final decisions are validated through the Sales Operations Portal and aligned with Routing Guidelines before execution.
